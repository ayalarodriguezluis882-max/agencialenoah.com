SERVICES

Four service lines, one standard of certified agents.

Whether it's a car, a container, or a one-time consultation: that's how we handle every type of operation at Lenoah.

SERVICE 01

Vehicle Importation

You bought a car in the United States and need to bring it to Mexico without surprises. We calculate your taxes, verify the vehicle is eligible for import, and handle the complete process until you have your plates.

  • ISAN, DTA, and VAT calculation before you decide to buy
  • Eligibility verification and NOM requirements
  • Complete import customs declaration processing
  • Temporary or permanent import plate management
  • Support on crossing day
SERVICE 02

Merchandise & Commercial Freight

For businesses that import on a recurring basis: full containers, consolidated freight (LTL), or full truckload (FTL). We classify and document your merchandise from the origin, not just once it's at the border.

  • Handling of full containers and LTL/FTL freight
  • Tariff classification from the country of origin
  • Review of non-tariff regulations and restrictions
  • Coordination with your supplier or freight forwarder
  • Shipment tracking through the crossing
SERVICE 03

Comprehensive Customs Management

Complete support throughout your operation: we generate the customs declaration, schedule your crossing appointment, and track it in real time, whether you get green or red lane, until your merchandise clears customs.

  • Customs declaration generation and validation
  • Crossing appointment scheduling
  • Real-time customs status tracking
  • Personalized support if your operation is selected for inspection
  • Complete file delivery at closing
SERVICE 04

Foreign Trade Consulting

For businesses that want to plan their import before moving a single box: consultative classification, applicable regulations, and strategy for operating recurringly at the border.

  • Consultative tariff classification
  • Identification of applicable non-tariff regulations
  • Import strategy for recurring operations
  • Guidance on sector registries and trading company services
