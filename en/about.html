ABOUT US

Foreign trade with real experience, crossing after crossing.

Lenoah is a customs brokerage and foreign trade consortium that operates every day at the Tijuana–San Diego border. This is what drives us.

TRACK RECORD

We learned the border checkpoint by operating it, not just by reading the regulations.

Lenoah was born at one of the busiest commercial crossings in North America: the Tijuana–San Diego border. There we understood that foreign trade isn't solved just with paperwork at a desk — it's solved by knowing the real timing, lanes, and rules of customs by heart.

That operational experience, earned crossing after crossing, is what we put behind every vehicle, container, or shipment we manage. We don't improvise with your merchandise: we treat it with the same care we'd give our own.

Agents with an active customs license, sector registries in order, and a team that follows your operation from start to finish — that's what backs every customs declaration we sign.
