FIRST TIME IMPORTING?

6 things you should know before importing.

What first-time importers almost always ask us.

01

Check permits and NOM requirements before buying

Some products require a prior permit or must comply with an Official Mexican Standard (NOM). Check this before closing the purchase, not after.

02

Gather a complete commercial invoice

Detailed description, value, quantity, and country of origin. An incomplete invoice is the number one cause of delays.

03

Budget for taxes, not just the purchase price

IGI, DTA, and VAT are added to the product cost. Get your estimate before buying, not when you arrive at the border.

04

Confirm if you need a sector registry

Certain merchandise requires a specific sector registry. We have an extensive catalog of active registries, and we confirm this for you before quoting.

05

Schedule your crossing appointment in advance

Especially during peak season. A scheduled appointment avoids wasted hours at the border.

06

Keep your complete file

Customs declaration, invoice, and COVE. These help with future clarifications or a faster next import.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What people ask us most.

If your question isn't here, write to us and we'll gladly answer it.

Do I need an importer registry to bring merchandise into Mexico?+

Yes, in most cases you need to be enrolled in the Importers Registry, and for certain products, also in a Specific Sectors Importers Registry. If you don't have either, Lenoah can operate as a trading company and import under your name.

How long does the complete import process take?+

It depends on the type of merchandise and whether your declaration is selected for customs inspection (physical review) or free clearance. Once documentation is complete, processing and crossing are usually resolved within a couple of business days.

What taxes will I pay when importing?+

Generally, the General Import Tax (IGI), Customs Processing Fee (DTA), and VAT (IVA) are calculated. For vehicles, the New Vehicle Tax (ISAN) is also added. We give you an estimate before you decide to move forward.

Can I import as an individual, without a company?+

Yes, depending on the type and value of the merchandise. In several cases, we can also support you with our trading company service if you don't have the required registry.

What happens if my declaration is selected for customs inspection (red light)?+

A certified agent accompanies you during the physical inspection. That's why we make sure documentation and tariff classification are correct from the start: it reduces the risk of issues.

Do you work with any type of merchandise?+

We handle vehicles, commercial freight, and general merchandise. For sectors regulated under specific registries, we confirm with you before quoting to make sure we can handle your import.
